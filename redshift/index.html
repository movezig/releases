What it does

Redshift lives in your menu bar and gives you instant control over your display's warmth, brightness, and color balance. Set it once and forget it, or use the built-in scheduler to automatically shift your screen from day to night.

Red Shift

Overlay a warm red tint to cut blue light. Adjustable from 0 to 100.

Brightness

Dim your display below the system minimum for comfortable late-night use.

Channel Balancing

Fine-tune red, green, blue, and contrast individually for a picture that looks right to you.

Profiles

Save your favorite setups and recall them instantly. Comes with five built-in presets.

Scheduler

Automatically fade between a daytime and evening profile at the times you choose.

Privacy First

Fully local. No analytics, no data collection, nothing leaves your Mac.

Getting started

  1. Click Download Beta above to get Redshift.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag Redshift to your Applications folder
  3. The first time you open Redshift, macOS will show a warning: “Apple could not verify Redshift is free of malware.” This is normal for beta software that hasn’t been notarized yet. To proceed: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the Redshift message
  4. Approve the Screen Recording permission when macOS asks — this is needed for the display overlays and nothing is ever recorded or transmitted
  5. Look for the red/white circle in your menu bar — click it to open the control panel
